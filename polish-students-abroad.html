Polish students abroad: data

How many Polish students are enrolled at universities abroad, and in which countries? We collected the latest data from UNESCO, Eurostat, HESA, IIE Open Doors and FRSE. Every number comes with its year and source.

Key numbers

  • 21,460In 2024, 21,460 people from Poland were enrolled in full degree programmes abroad (UNESCO UIS).
  • 1.24%In 2024, 1.24% of Polish students studied abroad (UNESCO UIS).
  • −21%Since the 2021 peak (27,314 people) the number of Polish students abroad has fallen by 5,854, or 21% (UNESCO UIS, our calculation).
  • 12,737In 2024, 12,737 people from Poland studied in EU countries, most of them in Germany (4,989) and Denmark (1,403) (Eurostat).
  • −78%In 2024/25, 2,390 students from Poland were enrolled at UK universities, down from 10,755 in 2020/21 (HESA).
  • 2×The number of Polish students in Italy grew from 454 in 2020 to 975 in 2024, and in Sweden from 214 to 472 (Eurostat).
  • 1,809In 2024/25, 1,809 people from Poland were studying or on post-study practical training in the US, up from 1,661 a year earlier (IIE Open Doors).
  • 18,407In 2024, students of Polish universities went on 18,407 Erasmus+ exchanges (FRSE, 2024 report).

USA

In 2024/25 there were 1,809 people from Poland in the US: 936 undergraduates, 503 graduate students, 139 non-degree students and 231 on Optional Practical Training (OPT). Source: IIE Open Doors, Poland Fact Sheet 2025.

How to read these numbers

  • Degree mobility: a person completes a whole degree programme abroad. This is what UNESCO and Eurostat count.
  • Erasmus+ (credit mobility): a semester, year or traineeship abroad, with the degree from a Polish university. FRSE counts exchanges, not people, so one person can be counted twice.
  • We do not add up numbers from different sources, because each defines country of origin differently (secondary school, citizenship or residence).
  • Eurostat does not say whether "2024" means the 2023/24 or 2024/25 academic year, so we write "in 2024".
