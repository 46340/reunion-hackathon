Questions and answers.

Who can apply?
Polish students and graduates of universities abroad, mainly in computer science, AI/ML, engineering and data science. We select based on applications. We will publish the exact criteria together with the form.
How much does it cost?
Taking part is free. Places are limited to 100, with selection by application.
Do I need a team?
No. You can apply solo, and teams can also form on site during the opening.
Do I need to code?
The core of the event is building a prototype, but good teams also need design and business. What counts is what you bring to the project.
Do we work at night?
Yes. The room is open round the clock, from the start on Friday at 20:00 until projects are submitted on Sunday.
How does the project presentation work?
Each team has 3 minutes for the pitch and 2 minutes for questions from the jury. On Saturday at noon we run a pitching workshop.
When do applications open?
Soon. The form will appear on this page. Also follow the GPSS channels.
When and where is the event?
In Warsaw, 5–7 February 2027. We will announce the exact venue before applications open.
Does the organiser cover travel and accommodation?
We will share the logistics with accepted participants.
How do I become a partner?
Write to maks.rokosz@globalpolsoc.com. We will send you the partner deck with packages, benefits and rates. It is not available for download.
What language is the event in?
Polish. Some workshops and materials may be in English.
